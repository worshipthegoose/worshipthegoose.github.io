The Goose Site is Getting Deactivated

Our developer team, Goose Development Studios (written in The Goose Site as "the gosedevs") have decided to shutter The Goose Site's doors effective August 10. Many workers in Goose Development have experienced burnout, pushing over 700 commits on Github within a span of three months. This does not mean The Goose Site is gone forever. The team will return to normal operations at an unspecified time.

Frequently Asked

Is The Goose Site permanently gone?

No, we will return to The Goose Site someday but it is unclear when. Think of this as a hiatus.

Why can't the team leave The Goose Site running?

The Goose Site takes up around 10 gigabites of total space and took two business days to switch servers. When our team goes on hiatus, Github may take down our website from Github Pages as it is a lot of money to pay to keep their megaservers running, especially for a ten gig website. The devs ultimately decided to release this message and shut down the main site, while keeping the former domain open with this page only.
