Administrators: What are they?

Administrators, commonly shortened to admins and also known as goosadmins, are moderators who run, manage, and moderate The Goose Chatbox. These select gooselings can delete and comments, go anonymous under aliases, and gain a lot more perks compared to standard-level gooselings. This status is given by @thegoose, and it's estimated that only ten or so gooselings have administrator access.
A star icon (✦) next to a feature indicates that it is an admin perk. Star icons do not appear for standard-tier individuals.
Messages from admin accounts do not have the star icon next to their name to avoid unnecessary harassment.

Perks

Some, but not all, perks include:

  • Faster servers for faster messaging.

  • Twice the encryption compared to a normal gooseling account

  • The abillity to delete, edit, and clear all messages

  • Alias mode (going under an alias which cannot be an existing account)

  • Admin-only messaging

The Goose Chatbox Guidelines

List of guidelines and policies for The Goose Chatbox.

Some words or phrases on this page may be censored. To view the uncensored text, simply hover or look at the censored text a bit closer...

  • Basic Goosettiquete

    The Goose Chatbox Administrators will not tolerate hate speech or discriminatory conduct. This includes racism, sexism, misogyny, misandry, homophobia, queerphobia, cisheteronormativity, androcentrism, islamophobia, anti-religion remarks, ethinicity bullying, xenophobia, ageism, gender-based discrimination and sex-based discrimination.

  • Cross Site Scripting

    Using The Goose Chatbox for Cross Site Scripting (also known simply as XSS) is illegal under federal law. If any admin notices this behavior, (they will) you will be IP banned from the website and will be reported to the authorities.

  • No Explicit or Inappropriate Content

    Promoting or mentioning explicit or violent content (includes music) is an instant ban as most, if not all gooselings do not want to see or hear anything indecorous, which exclude goosism related material.

  • Zero Tolerance for Foul Language

    The use of any profanity, vulgarity, or prohibited language may result in the immediate termination of your account. There is zero tolerance for this type of behavior. If you really need to use profanity, replace the word with "goose."

  • Freedom of Speech

    While hatred or scorn of The Goose is discouraged, it is still allowed since The Goose Chatbox is a not a totalitarian state. Talks about politics are heavily discoruaged and should be talked about outside of The Goose Chatbox.

  • Stay on Topic: 80% of Messages Should Be Related to Geese or The Goose.

    It's important to know that messing around is sometimes tolerated, please keep it at an 80:20 ratio.

  • Messages Must Basic Goose Typing Rules

    Using the letter "2" when writing canon or talking about goose-related topics is not allowed. Canonically, "2" is a short form of The 2oose, who is The Goosatan. If you are writing "The 2oose", that is allowed. However, when saying the number, substitute it with "goose." (Note: If you are trying to type "The 2oose" in a place where numbers are not allowed, type "the qoose" since Q in cursive looks like a goose.

  • Consequences to Violation

    Violation of these rules stated above could cause a permanent ban, IP ban, or report to the local authorities. As stated in rule V, scorn of The Goose is never a violation.
