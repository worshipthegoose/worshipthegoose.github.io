Values

Before the conception of The Goose Site: Relaunch, most thought that the concept of the original website was distasteful and disgusting. Google refused to index our website, citing concerns of systematic psychological manipulation. The decision was made to not only change our site's overall UX and design but to update our core values.
The Goose Site started as a joke. The Goose Site will end as a hybrid informational-educational website.

There are 10 core values of The Goose Site: Relaunch. These do not apply to the original goose site.

  • The site cannot use, contain, or include works of artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

  • The site must have a readable and clean font.

  • The site has to be appropriate for all viewers' ages.

  • The site cannot contain references to religion or "Goosism."

  • The site must generate zero profit and be free of advertisements.
